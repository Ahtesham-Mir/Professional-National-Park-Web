WILDLIFE GUIDE

WILDLIFE OF PAKISTAN

Discover the magnificent creatures that call Pakistan home

Markhor

Capra falconeri

Endangered

The national animal of Pakistan, known for its magnificent spiral horns. Found in the mountainous regions of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Azad Kashmir.

🏔️ Habitat: Alpine forests and rocky cliffs
📏 Height: 65-115 cm at shoulder
🍃 Diet: Grasses, leaves, shrubs

Snow Leopard

Panthera uncia

Vulnerable

The ghost of the mountains, this elusive big cat roams the high-altitude regions of northern Pakistan. Known for its beautiful spotted coat and incredible adaptability.

🏔️ Habitat: High mountain ranges (3,000-5,000m)
📏 Length: 75-150 cm (body)
🍖 Diet: Blue sheep, ibex, small mammals

Himalayan Brown Bear

Ursus arctos isabellinus

Vulnerable

A subspecies of brown bear found in the Himalayan regions of Pakistan. These magnificent creatures play a crucial role in mountain ecosystems.

🏔️ Habitat: Alpine meadows and forests
⚖️ Weight: 130-550 kg
🍓 Diet: Omnivorous - plants, insects, small animals

Himalayan Ibex

Capra sibirica

Protected

A wild goat species with impressive curved horns, adapted to life in the harsh mountain environments of northern Pakistan.

🏔️ Habitat: Rocky mountain slopes
📏 Height: 70-100 cm at shoulder
🌿 Diet: Grasses, herbs, shrubs

Asian Black Bear

Ursus thibetanus

Vulnerable

Also known as the moon bear due to the distinctive white crescent on its chest. Found in the forested regions of northern Pakistan.

🌲 Habitat: Forested mountain areas
⚖️ Weight: 90-200 kg
🍯 Diet: Omnivorous - fruits, insects, honey

Marco Polo Sheep

Ovis ammon polii

Endangered

Named after the famous explorer, this wild sheep is known for its magnificent spiral horns that can reach up to 140 cm in length.

🏔️ Habitat: High-altitude plateaus
📏 Horn length: Up to 140 cm
🌾 Diet: Grasses, sedges, herbs
